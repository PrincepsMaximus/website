01. Public speaking

Public speaking is something I thoroughly enjoy, from conference talks to outreach activities. Communicating results clearly, be it to specialists, to colleagues at an internal seminar or to a broader audience, sharpens my own understanding of a topic, and the discussions that follow regularly raise interesting new research questions.

Below, you can find my upcoming and past talks, together with slides and recordings where available.

Beyond academia

Outside my own research area, I also enjoy bringing science to a broader audience, mainly within the Flemish amateur astronomy community:

  • >
    Coding Theory in Space Communications: a lecture on how error-correcting codes keep data from spacecraft intact, given in a course at Volkssterrenwacht Beisbroek, the public observatory and planetarium of Bruges, and in a shorter version at the JVS/VVS-weekend (2025).
  • >
    JVS Hyperion: monthly talks for this local youth astronomy club, part of the JVS, the youth branch of the Vereniging Voor Sterrenkunde (VVS).
  • >
    VVS Summer School on Astronomy: practical sessions at the summer school for secondary-school students that the VVS organises in Leuven, together with the Flemish universities.
