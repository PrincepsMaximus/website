status: PhD Researcher @ COSIC, KU Leuven

Working towards openly verifiable perceptual hash functions.

I focus on the cryptographic aspects of perceptual hash functions, specifically the formalisation of their properties, as well as their evaluation and design. Current technologies, widely used for content moderation and detecting copyright infringement, often rely on security-through-obscurity. My research pursues the design of openly verifiable algorithms, which I believe are a strict requirement for large-scale deployments.

01. About

I am a first-year PhD student at COSIC, KU Leuven, interested in perceptual hash functions from a cryptographic and mathematical perspective. In particular, I focus on designing openly verifiable systems rather than relying on security-through-obscurity. I am fortunate to be advised by Prof. Bart Preneel.

Previously, I obtained a Master’s degree in Mathematics from Ghent University. In my thesis, supervised by Prof. Jan De Beule (Vrije Universiteit Brussel) and Prof. Leo Storme (Ghent University), I studied cryptographic topics using techniques from finite geometry. Through this work, I was able to sharpen the lower bound on the number of non-bent components of crooked quadratic (APN) functions.
